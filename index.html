Before you start

Common
questions.

Is this legal advice?

These kits help organize an internal HR review. Verify findings against your records and take legal questions, notices or disputes to qualified employment counsel.

Who sees my employee data?

We never receive the records you use with a kit. Your chosen AI provider processes whatever you submit under your account settings and terms. Use employer approved tools, remove identifying details and start with fictional records.

Do I need to be technical?

You need your own Claude or ChatGPT account and the ability to open a zip file and follow instructions in a chat. There is no separate software to install or seller account to manage.

What happens when the law changes?

Treat each download as a dated edition. Check current official guidance and consult counsel about changes that affect your business. Timing and pricing for future editions will be confirmed at launch.

Can I buy the kits today?

The collection is in preview. You can explore every kit and its sample workflow now. Checkout will open once the files and purchase terms are ready.
