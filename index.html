Fresno store payroll review

Sample review on fictional test data.

Workforce
15 employees
Review result
7 fixes identified

Payroll was short by $371.63

Reconcile the affected pay entries against time and pay records. Confirm the correction with the payroll provider.

One final check was 9 days late

Verify the departure date and the payment date. Give employment counsel the timeline to review.

One salaried manager fell below the California exempt minimum

Gather the salary history and job duties. Ask employment counsel to review the classification and any pay implications.

Common questions

Is this legal advice?

No. The kits help you review records and prepare questions. Take decisions about employee classification, pay obligations, or legal exposure to qualified employment counsel.

Who can see my employee data?

The review runs in your own AI account. We never receive your employee records. Your provider’s privacy settings and any sharing permissions on your workspace apply.

Do I need to be technical?

You need to be able to open a ZIP, add the skill to your account, and upload a file. The kit walks you through the review. You can start with one payroll run or one policy.

Can I use ChatGPT instead of Claude?

Yes. The skills are built for Claude, and the kits can also be used in your own ChatGPT account.

What happens when the law changes?

A downloaded kit reflects its edition. Check current requirements with counsel when they affect a decision. An optional paid California update is under consideration. Its timing and price are still open.
