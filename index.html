Before you start

Good questions.

Is this legal advice?

These kits help you organize an internal HR review. Verify findings against your records and bring legal questions, notices or disputes to qualified employment counsel.

Who sees my employee data?

We never receive the records you use with the kit. Your AI provider processes the information you submit under its terms and your account settings. Start with fictional records and remove identifying details before using an employer approved account.

Do I need to be technical?

You need your own Claude or ChatGPT account and the ability to open a zip file and follow instructions in a chat. Everything runs in the AI account you already use.

What happens when the law changes?

Each download is a dated edition. Check current official guidance and consult counsel about changes that affect your business. Timing and pricing for future editions will be confirmed at launch.

When can I buy the kits?

The collection is in preview. Checkout will open once the downloadable files and purchase terms are ready.
