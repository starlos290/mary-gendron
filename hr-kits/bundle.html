Before you start

Browse all review kits

Do I need to run the kits together?

You can run each review on its own. For example, start with a single pay period and come back to your handbook when you have the right records.

Where do my employee records go?

Upload records directly to your own Claude or ChatGPT account. HR Fieldwork never receives your employee data. Your AI provider’s settings and terms apply.

Is this legal advice?

No. These are educational tools for reviewing HR records. Bring questions about legal obligations, classification, or individual cases to qualified employment counsel.
