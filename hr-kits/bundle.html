Inside the bundle

Each kit includes the skill, checklists, templates and an Excel workbook. Choose the review you need and run it in your own Claude or ChatGPT account.

Payroll Review
A report comparing payroll entries with time records, including differences to investigate with your payroll provider.
HR and Handbook Audit
A prioritized list of gaps between your written policies, HR files and workplace practices.
California Compliance
A review of the records that support your California HR practices and the questions to take to employment counsel.

When to use the bundle

Use Payroll Review to trace a corrected time entry to the pay register. Use the handbook kit to check who is supposed to approve that correction. Then use the California kit to document the follow up.
