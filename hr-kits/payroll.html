Kit contents

Inside the payroll review

Start with one pay period. Keep a copy of the records your payroll provider used so you can compare the same dates.

What you bring

A payroll register and matching time records. Use consistent employee codes wherever a name is unnecessary.

What you review

Hours, pay entries and differences between the two records. Missing information becomes a question for the person who owns the record.

What you take away

A list of entries to verify, the records behind each question, and a next step for your payroll provider.

Fictional example

Two records, two hour totals.

A fictional employee appears in both records for the same week. The entries give the owner a place to start.

Illustration of the review process. Actual skill output can vary.

Pay period review notes

Read the two entries

The time record for employee P04 shows 32 hours for the week. The payroll register shows 30 hours for the same employee and dates.

Identify the missing detail

The difference is two hours. The records supplied do not show whether a correction, an adjustment, or a different payroll cutoff explains it.

Take the question to payroll

“Can you show me which time entries went into this pay run for P04 and whether any hours moved to another period?”
