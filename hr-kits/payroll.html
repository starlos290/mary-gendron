Sample walkthrough

Where did the two hours go?

An owner notices that two records for the same person show different totals. Follow the entries from source to payroll.

01Match the records

Check that employee P04 and the pay period match on both records. Compare the original entries with the payroll export.

02Check the adjustment history

The two hour difference needs an explanation. Check for a correction, an adjustment or a different cutoff before deciding what happened.

03Ask payroll for the missing detail

Ask the provider which entries fed this run and whether any hours moved to another pay period. Save their answer alongside the two records.

What goes into a pay run review?

Bring the source records

Gather the payroll register and the matching time records. Use the same employee codes in both files.

Work through the entries

Review hours and pay entries for one period at a time. Keep incomplete records on the list of open questions.

Hand off the question

Give your provider the employee code, pay period, entry and exact difference to investigate.
