Sample walkthrough

Was the session documented?

A California owner remembers holding supervisor training. Start with what the file can show, then assign the question that remains.

01Identify what is on file

The calendar invitation and the agenda identify the session that was scheduled for October 8.

02Find the missing evidence

Neither item records who attended. Ask the training coordinator whether a roster, acknowledgment or separate completion record exists.

03Record who will follow up

Give the coordinator the session date and the documents already reviewed. Bring questions about applicable training requirements to employment counsel.

What goes into an employer review?

Describe your workplace

Start with locations, workforce details and the practices being reviewed. Gather the records you already use.

Check the evidence

Review records of payroll checks, policy distribution, supervisor training and corrective action. Note who owns each record.

Give open issues an owner

Track evidence to locate and questions to verify. Ask qualified employment counsel about applicable California or federal requirements.
