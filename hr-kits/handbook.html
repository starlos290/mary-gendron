Kit contents

Inside the handbook audit

A handbook can look complete on paper while a manager follows a different process. The review puts the two accounts side by side.

What you bring

A handbook section, the related HR file or form, and a brief account of how the team handles that task today.

What you review

The written instruction, the reported practice, and the records available to support each one.

What you take away

A prioritized list of discrepancies to verify with managers, records to locate, and policy questions to discuss with counsel.

Handbook review notes

Read the handbook entry

The handbook directs employees to submit a time off request to the office manager using a form.

Compare it with practice

A supervisor reports that staff usually send a text to their shift lead. The materials provided do not say who passes those requests to the office manager.

Choose the first follow up

Ask the office manager and shift lead to trace a recent request together. Once the actual process is clear, the owner can decide what needs updating and which questions need counsel.
