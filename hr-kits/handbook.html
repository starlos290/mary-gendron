Sample walkthrough

Follow one time off request.

An owner hears two different explanations for how staff request time off. The documents give them a place to start.

01Read the policy

The handbook asks employees to send a form to the office manager. Pull the current form and confirm which handbook edition the team received.

02Ask how the shift runs

The shift lead says staff usually send a text. Ask them to walk through what happens after a message arrives.

03Choose the first follow up

Ask the shift lead and office manager to trace a recent request together. Once the path is clear, decide which instructions or records need attention.

What goes into a handbook audit?

Bring one operating process

Gather the handbook section, the relevant form or file, and a manager’s account of the current practice.

Compare policy with practice

Look at the written instruction, the reported practice, and the record that supports each one.

Prioritize the follow up

List the gaps to verify, who can answer each question, and which policy issues to discuss with counsel.
